## Design Context

### Users
Oria is for creators, builders, and technical teams who publish large files, media bundles, archives, or datasets through Shelby-backed storage. They need a dApp that feels like a polished product first, with wallet and network details appearing only when they matter.

### Brand Personality
Refined, quiet, and capable. Oria should not feel overtly crypto or web3. It should feel like a premium creative tool for storing, publishing, and sharing substantial digital work.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm graphite and ivory with restrained teal and clay accents. The interface should take inspiration from smooth, clean product pages with subtle motion, disciplined spacing, crisp type, light texture, and strong product previews.

### Design Principles
- Make Shelby Testnet and Shelbynet visible as product-quality environments, not raw protocol toggles.
- Treat upload, signing, network selection, and publishing states as designed experiences.
- Use motion sparingly: opacity and transform only, with calm easing and reduced-motion support.
- Keep the brand non-web3: no coins, chains, hexagons, neon dashboards, or generic cloud metaphors.
- Prefer spacious product surfaces, editorial rhythm, and precise interaction detail over dense marketing copy.
