## Design Context

### Users
Oria is built for Shelby and Aptos creators, collectors, and early protocol testers who publish or unlock media-heavy Spaces on Shelbynet first, then Shelby Testnet once access is available.

### Brand Personality
Calm, premium, precise. The interface should feel like a serious creator tool rather than a protocol playground.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined light product surface with tactile paper, restrained teal/clay accents, strong typography, and careful motion. The app should communicate trust, authorship, and on-chain clarity without looking like a generic Web3 dashboard.

### Design Principles
- Make every product state feel intentional: publish, unlock, receipt, sale, and preview states should read as real workflows.
- Avoid demo language, placeholder labels, and raw technical errors in user-facing screens.
- Preserve Shelby/Aptos specificity, but explain it in creator-friendly language.
- Keep the UI calm and premium: fewer words, clearer hierarchy, stable spacing, and purposeful motion only.
